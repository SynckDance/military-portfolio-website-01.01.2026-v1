PERSONNEL FILE // CLEARANCE LEVEL OMEGA

SINCLAIR OGAGA EMOGHENE

ASSISTANT PROFESSOR · CHOREOGRAPHER · RESEARCHER

University of Texas at Austin sinclair.dance [email protected] ORCID: 0000-0001-9362-3992

My research investigates the intersections of dance, technology, and cultural memory, focusing on how embodied knowledge is archived, transmitted, and reactivated through digital, material, and affective systems. Central to my work are projects such as Global Movement Research (GMR) and The Thinking Archive, which examine the transformation of archival practice through artificial embodied intelligence (AEI), motion capture, and ethical design.
